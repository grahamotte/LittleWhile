# Little While Support

Little While is a focus timer for iPhone running iOS 18 or later.

## Get in touch

For help, questions, or bug reports, email Graham Otte at [go@graham.lol](mailto:go@graham.lol). Include your iOS version and a brief description of what happened. Screenshots are helpful, but please remove any private information before sending them.

## Start a timer

Open Settings using the sliders button, choose a theme and your focus and rest durations, then tap Start. You can pause and resume from the main timer screen.

## Completion alerts

Allow notifications when prompted. On iOS 26 or later, Little While can also request alarm permission so completion alarms can sound during Silent mode or a Focus. You can manage these permissions in iPhone Settings. If alarm access is unavailable, Little While uses local notifications when permitted.

Live Activities show your timer on the Lock Screen and, on supported iPhones, the Dynamic Island. Check Little While's Live Activities setting in iPhone Settings if they do not appear.

## Run history

Tap the history button to see your saved runs and monthly activity calendar. Your settings and history stay on your device. You can delete individual past runs from History. Removing the app deletes its local data.

## Privacy

Little While has no account, analytics, advertising, or tracking. Read the [Privacy Policy](privacy-policy.md) for details.
