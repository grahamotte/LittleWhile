# Little While Support

Little While is a Pomodoro focus and rest timer for iPhone running iOS 18 or later. Work in cycles of focus and rest, loop them automatically, follow the countdown from your Lock Screen, and see your focus time add up on a monthly calendar.

## Get in touch

## Start a timer

Open Settings using the sliders button, choose a theme and your focus and rest durations, then tap Start. Focus can be 1 to 120 minutes. Rest can be 0 to 120 minutes; set it to 0 for a single focus block with no break. When focus ends, Little While alerts you and moves straight into rest.

History and Settings are hidden while a run is active. Stop the run to get back to them. Touch and hold the play button to start a run over.

## Loop focus and rest

Turn on Loop in Settings before tapping Start. When rest ends, the next focus block starts automatically, and focus and rest keep repeating until you tap stop. History shows how many cycles each looped run completed.

## Pausing

Pausing is intentionally short. A paused timer shows a one-minute countdown and then resumes on its own, so a quick interruption doesn't turn into an abandoned session. Tap play to resume sooner, or tap stop to end the run. Stopped runs keep their progress in History.

## Themes

Pick a theme at the top of Settings:

- **Mr Smiles**: a smiley face that bounces around the screen while you work. Drag and flick him while the timer runs.
- **He Loves Me**: a daisy that drops its petals one by one as time passes.
- **Boring**: a minimal countdown.

## Completion alerts

Allow notifications when prompted. On iOS 26 or later, Little While can also request alarm permission so completion alarms can sound during Silent mode or a Focus, at the end of each focus and rest period. You can manage these permissions in iPhone Settings. If alarm access is unavailable, Little While uses local notifications when permitted.

## Lock Screen and Dynamic Island

Live Activities show your remaining time and whether you are in focus or rest on the Lock Screen and, on supported iPhones, the Dynamic Island. Check Little While's Live Activities setting in iPhone Settings if they do not appear.

## Run history

Tap the history button to see your saved runs and monthly activity calendar. Each day's dot grows with that day's focus time; use the arrows or swipe to move between months. Your settings and history stay on your device. You can delete individual past runs from History. Removing the app deletes its local data.

## Privacy

Little While has no account, analytics, advertising, or tracking. Read the [Privacy Policy](privacy-policy.md) for details.
